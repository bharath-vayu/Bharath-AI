Small utilities that run in your browser or on your Windows machine. No accounts, no installs.

Transcriber

Live meeting transcription with timed screenshots. Hears the other participants through a screen share and you through the microphone, then exports Text, HTML, PDF or Word, optionally straight to Google Drive.

  • Browser: Chrome or Edge. Live words as people speak, remote voices labeled Speaker 1, Speaker 2, ... Nothing leaves your machine unless you save to Drive.
  • Windows app: same features, fully offline, no share prompt, higher accuracy. Unzip and run.
Open in browser Download for Windows
Tip: open it in Chrome or Edge, then use the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or menu → Install Transcriber) to get it as a desktop app with its own window and Start menu entry. It keeps updating from this site.
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" for the download because it is unsigned. More info, Run anyway.
