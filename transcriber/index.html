Help

How it works.

Recording

Press Start recording. In the share dialog choose Entire Screen and keep Share system audio on so the other participants are heard (a window share carries no audio on Windows). Allow the microphone when asked.

Grey words are live and provisional. When a speaker pauses, the statement becomes a row with a speaker label; the newest row never changes while you read it, and the cleaner rewrite lands when the next statement starts.

Speakers

Remote voices are labeled Speaker 1, Speaker 2, ... by voice, and a statement is split where the voice changes. Two people speaking at the exact same instant cannot be separated. When you are physically in the room with the others, turn the microphone off so you are not heard twice.

Saving

Stop shows Text, HTML, PDF and Word. With Google Drive connected the session is also saved automatically and appears under My recordings on every device where you sign in.

Phones

Phones cannot capture the screen or system audio, so the page switches to microphone only. Put the phone where it hears everyone.

Machines

The speech models adapt to the machine: the larger model on a real graphics card, the lighter one elsewhere; the status line says which is running. First use downloads them once.
